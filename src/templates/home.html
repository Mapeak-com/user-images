{{host}}the image store behind Mapeak

The photos people pin to places on the map are kept here.

When someone adds a picture to a place on Mapeak, this service stores it, and the place on OpenStreetMap keeps the picture's address in its image tag. There is nothing to browse at this address. Every picture is reached from the place it belongs to.
